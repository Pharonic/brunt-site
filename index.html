BRUNT

Solo Survivor

One hero, one island, and everything on it coming for you. Hold the Fallen Reach, take an upgrade every level, and decide each act whether to bank what you have or push your luck for more.

Coming to iPhone. Built for a phone held upright, playable with one thumb, and playable offline.

What it is

  • A survivor round that runs about ten minutes, and ends when you say so.
  • Two heroes with kits that play nothing alike, and card collections you set before a run.
  • A rank ladder that remembers how far you got.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, and nothing to connect to. It is just a game.

Buying things

There is one optional purchase and it sells hero looks. Appearance only, no stat advantage, and nothing about it is on a timer.
