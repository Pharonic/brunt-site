BRUNT

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

BRUNT is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no social features, and it does not ask you for your name, your email address or any other personal detail. This page explains the little data that does move, and where it goes.

What stays on your device

Your progress is saved on your phone and nowhere else. That includes your rank and medal points, the gold you have earned, which hero, cards and looks you have equipped, your sound and movement settings, and your run history. None of it is uploaded, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

BRUNT offers one optional in-app purchase, which unlocks cosmetic hero looks. It changes appearance only and confers no gameplay advantage.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details, your Apple ID or your billing address.

To know whether you have bought the item — including on a new phone, or after reinstalling — the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated identifier for your installation together with the purchase receipt Apple issues. That identifier is not your name, your email or your Apple ID, it is not linked to your identity, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites. It exists so that "Restore purchases" works.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking software.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to sell.

Children

BRUNT is suitable for a general audience and contains cartoon fantasy violence. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can erase everything the game has stored by using Reset Save Data in the game's Options screen, or by deleting the app. To ask about the purchase record held by Apple or RevenueCat, write to us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
