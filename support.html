Support

Something wrong, or just a question? Write to pharonic.h@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the person who made the game.

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong.

I bought the bundle and my looks are gone

Open Options and tap Restore purchases, or open the Shop and tap Restore purchases under the welcome offer. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. The looks then appear under Armory → Skins.

I reinstalled and lost my progress

Progress is saved on the phone itself, so deleting the app deletes your rank, gold and run history. A purchase is not lost that way — restore it as above. There is no cloud save yet.

I was charged twice

The bundle is a one-time purchase and Apple will not charge twice for it; buying again on the same Apple ID restores it at no cost. For anything that looks like a real double charge, refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Write to us as well and we will help you chase it.

The game runs badly or closes on its own

Close other apps and try again, and make sure iOS is up to date. If it keeps happening, tell us your phone model and what was on screen. Turning on Reduce screen shake & effects in Options also lightens the load.

How do I play?

Touch anywhere and drag to steer. Your hero attacks on their own. Survive the waves, pick upgrades as you level, and at the end of an act decide whether to bank what you have or push on for more.

Is the purchase pay-to-win?

No. Everything for sale is appearance only, and nothing you can buy makes your hero stronger.
